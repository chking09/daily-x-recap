# Product Steering for Daily Market Recap

## Product Vision
A simple, automated tool that generates witty, educational daily market recaps for beginner/intermediate investors and posts them to X (Twitter).

## Core Value Proposition
**"Market insights made accessible"** - Transform complex financial data into digestible, engaging content that educates while informing.

## Target User
- **Primary**: Beginner to intermediate investors
- **Secondary**: Anyone interested in accessible market commentary
- **Not targeting**: Professional traders, institutional investors, or advanced analysts

## Content Standards

### Tone and Voice
- **Witty but not flippant**: Clever observations without making light of serious market events
- **Educational but not condescending**: Explain concepts without talking down to readers
- **Accessible but not oversimplified**: Use plain English while maintaining accuracy
- **Engaging but not sensational**: Interesting without being clickbait

### Content Structure (Non-negotiable)
1. **Market News Summary**: 2-3 sentences on major market events
2. **3 Stock Movers**: Companies that made significant moves with brief explanations
3. **3 Stocks to Watch**: Companies worth monitoring with reasoning
4. **Educational Context**: Brief insights woven throughout

### Quality Gates
- All stock picks must be mid-large cap (>$2B market cap)
- Explanations must be understandable to beginners
- Content must fit X platform constraints
- No financial advice or specific buy/sell recommendations
- Include disclaimers about educational nature

## Success Metrics (Simple)
- **Primary**: Daily recap posted successfully to X
- **Secondary**: Content quality (manual review)
- **Not measuring**: Engagement, followers, clicks, or other vanity metrics

## Feature Boundaries

### What We Build
- Automated data collection and processing
- Content generation with consistent quality
- Reliable posting to X platform
- Basic error handling and logging

### What We Don't Build
- User interfaces or dashboards
- Analytics or performance tracking
- Multiple content formats or platforms
- User interaction or response handling
- Content scheduling beyond basic automation

## Decision Framework
Before any product decision, ask:
1. Does this help beginners understand the market better?
2. Does this maintain the witty, educational tone?
3. Can this be automated without human intervention?
4. Does this fit within X platform constraints?

If any answer is "no", don't build it.
